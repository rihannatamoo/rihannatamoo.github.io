THE PRODUCT THESIS

The useful unit was not an AI model. It was a better decision.

The strategy became clearer when I framed AI around the work users were already doing. The model mattered only when it reduced interpretation effort, surfaced useful context and improved the next action.

Business data→AI analysis→Relevant signal→Decision context→Business action
PRODUCT PORTFOLIO LOGIC

Different user jobs required different product experiences.

DOCUMENTS

Interpret complex information

Reduce the effort required to extract important facts, compare information and identify what deserves attention.

FINANCIAL

Prioritize material signals

Help business users identify movement, exceptions and context that matter for a financial or operating decision.

ANALYTICS

Explain what changed

Move beyond static visualization by connecting patterns to a clear explanation of why the user should care.

WORKFLOW

Move insight into action

Design around the decision and follow through, rather than treating the dashboard as the end of the product.
