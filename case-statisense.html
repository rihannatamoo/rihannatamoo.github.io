THE PRODUCT IDEA

The value was not the AI model. It was the decision the product improved.

I framed AI around work users were already trying to complete. The technology mattered when it reduced interpretation effort, surfaced the right context and made the next decision clearer.

Business data→AI analysis→Relevant signal→Decision context→Business action
PRODUCT DIRECTION

Each use case started with a business job, not a generic AI feature.

DOCUMENTS

Make complex information easier to interpret

Extract the material facts from long documents and organize them around the questions a business user needs to answer.

FINANCIAL

Surface the signals that matter

Help users identify meaningful movement, exceptions and context in financial reports or market information.

BORROWER

Support a faster review

Bring relevant borrower information and risk signals into a structured view that supports analysis and decisioning.

PAYMENTS

Find exceptions and next actions

Use invoice and payment information to surface issues, reconciliation context and the items that require attention.

MY PRODUCT LEADERSHIP

Turn company direction into product choices a team could build and a customer could understand.

Use case definitionTranslate “AI analytics” into specific business problems, users and jobs to be done.
Workflow designDefine how a user moves from source information to insight, context and a next decision.
PrioritizationFocus product work on use cases where AI could remove meaningful effort or decision friction.
Product narrativeExplain the direction clearly to internal teams, customers and external audiences.
