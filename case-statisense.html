CASE STUDY 03 · HEAD OF PRODUCTS · AI ANALYTICS · DATA PRODUCTS

Built an AI analytics product direction around better business decisions, not more dashboards.

At Statisense, I led product work as the company expanded from data visualization into AI assisted analytics. The product opportunity was to help business users move from raw data and documents to relevant signals, context and action across financial analysis, document intelligence and business analytics workflows.

THE PRODUCT THESIS

The useful unit was not an AI model. It was a better business decision.

The product direction moved beyond collecting and visualizing data. AI was valuable when it reduced the effort required to interpret complex information and gave the user enough context to act with confidence.

VALUE CHAINData → Signal → Context → ActionThe model only matters if the workflow improves.
Raw data / document→AI analysis→Relevant signal→Decision context→Business action
WHAT WE BUILT AROUND

Concrete business jobs instead of generic “AI analytics.”

DOCUMENTS

Reduce interpretation time

Help users extract the important information from complex documents without manually reviewing every page and detail first.

FINANCIAL DATA

Surface material signals

Make it easier to identify trends, anomalies and decision relevant context in financial and operating information.

BUSINESS DATA

Move beyond dashboards

Turn data into prioritized explanations that help a user understand what changed and why it may matter.

ACTION

Connect insight to workflow

Design the experience around what the user does after seeing the analysis, not around displaying another visualization.

PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

Organize analysis around questions a business user can actually answer.
