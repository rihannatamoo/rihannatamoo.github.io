Helping move Statisense from data visualization toward AI powered decision products.

THE INFLECTION POINT

The business had credibility in data. The product challenge was turning that credibility into software people would use.

Statisense was known for data visualization and analytics. As demand for AI driven analytics accelerated, the company began moving beyond data presentation toward AI products for business users.

The trap to avoid: treating “AI” as the product. A viable product still needs a clear user, a high value decision or workflow, trustworthy outputs and a reason to become part of the operating process.
MY ROLE

I worked between company direction and product reality.

01

Translate the AI ambition

Frame where AI could remove decision friction in financial analysis, document analysis and business intelligence rather than pursue generic capability.

02

Move from information to action

Shape experiences around the decision a user needed to make, the context needed to trust the output and the next action the product should enable.

03

Use industry contexts to sharpen value

Banking, insurance, markets and document analysis turned an abstract AI story into concrete jobs to be done.

04

Represent the product narrative

Explain externally why the opportunity went beyond collecting and visualizing data and toward tools that help businesses act.

PRODUCT THESIS

The useful unit was not “an AI model.” It was a better business decision.

Recreated product value chain
Messy data / document→AI analysis→Relevant signal→Decision context→Business action
Public safe reconstruction of the product logic, not a proprietary Statisense screen.
FEATURED COVERAGE

The product narrative was independently visible and attributable.

On September 19, 2023, multiple technology and business publications covered Statisense’s AI transition and named me as Head of Products. This provides independent evidence of both the leadership role and the strategic product context.

My public product position: the opportunity was bigger than data collection and visualization; the goal was to build transformational tools for everyday businesses.
