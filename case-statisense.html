CASE STUDY 03 · HEAD OF PRODUCTS · AI STRATEGY · DATA PRODUCTS

Moving from data visualization toward AI products built around business decisions.

Statisense had strong visibility and credibility in data. The strategic question was how to turn that position into AI products businesses would repeatedly use. My role was to help move the story from “we have AI” to concrete decisions, workflows, use cases and product value.

01 · THE INFLECTION POINT

The company had earned attention through data. The next product had to earn repeated use.

Statisense was known for data visualization, analytics and data services. As demand for AI driven analytics accelerated, the business began moving beyond presenting information toward products that could help organizations interpret data and act faster.

EXISTING STRENGTH

Data credibility and reach

The brand had a strong public data presence and a meaningful audience. That created trust and distribution, but it did not automatically define the next product.

MARKET SHIFT

Generative and analytical AI

Businesses increasingly expected software to synthesize, explain and recommend, not only visualize information.

PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY

Decision support

Complex datasets and documents could become easier to understand when the product focused on a specific business decision.

PRODUCT RISK

AI as a vague feature set

Without a clear user and decision, “AI analytics” could become impressive technology without a durable workflow or reason to return.

The strategic shift I helped frame:

Move from “we show data” toward “we help a business user understand the signal, trust the interpretation and take the next action.”

02 · FROM COMPANY AMBITION TO PRODUCT STRATEGY

I translated a broad AI direction into a value chain the product team could use.

The strategy became more concrete when I stopped treating the model as the center of the experience. The useful unit was a better business decision. That changed the roadmap conversation from “what can the model generate?” to “which decisions are slow, expensive or inconsistent today, and what evidence does the user need to act with confidence?”

Recreated decision value chain
Raw data or document→AI analysis→Relevant signal→Decision context→Human judgment→Business action

03 · USE CASE FRAMING

Industry context turned “AI for business” into jobs users could recognize.

Publicly announced focus areas included banking, insurance, identity discovery, financial market insights and document analysis. I used specific contexts like these to make the proposition sharper and to expose the workflow, trust and data questions hidden inside a generic AI story.

04 · PRODUCT THESIS

The model was infrastructure. The product was the decision experience around it.

Principle
Why it mattered
Product implication
Outcome before output
A long generated answer is not automatically useful.
Design around the decision, task or follow up the user needs to complete.
Context before confidence
Users cannot trust AI just because the interface looks certain.
Show enough source context and reasoning structure for the user to evaluate the result.
Specificity before breadth
“AI for everyone” makes prioritization and positioning weak.
Use concrete industry problems to sharpen product value and requirements.
Capability before one surface
Reusable intelligence can create value through both products and APIs.
Separate underlying capability from the channel through which customers consume it.
05 · STRATEGY INTO PRODUCT MECHANICS

I worked at both levels: the story executives needed and the detail product teams needed.

STRATEGIC LEVEL

Why should this product exist?

  • Target business problem
  • User and buyer
  • Value proposition
  • Portfolio fit
  • Product and API opportunity
PRODUCT LEVEL

What must the experience actually do?

  • Input and source context
  • Analysis and interpretation
  • Trust and review
  • Decision workflow
  • Action and measurable outcome

That connection is important because strategy becomes weak when it cannot survive contact with workflows, edge cases and implementation constraints. I used the product thesis to keep use cases coherent while still allowing the underlying intelligence to be reused across multiple surfaces.

06 · INDEPENDENT PUBLIC PROOF

The product narrative was public, attributable and independently published.

On September 19, 2023, TechCabal, Nairametrics and TechEconomy covered Statisense’s AI transition. The coverage identified me in product leadership and connected my role to the strategic move beyond data collection and visualization.

07 · OUTCOMES AND MEASUREMENT

I connect strategic direction to operating value, not only launch language.

Product work associated with the analytics experience contributed to an approximately 20% improvement in approval and analytics efficiency. I treat that result separately from the public AI transition itself because a strategic narrative and an operating metric are different forms of evidence.

I can turn a strategic technology shift into a product direction people can understand, build and believe.
