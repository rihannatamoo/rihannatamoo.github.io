EVIDENCE 04 · AI WORKFLOW & ADOPTION

Redesigning document work around the moments users actually valued.

A product story about applying AI where it creates practical leverage: reducing repetitive document work, improving the relevance of AI output, and strengthening adoption through better workflows and enterprise foundations.

RoleProduct Manager
DomainAI document intelligence
Primary impact50%+ documentation time
Adoption+35% feature adoption
THE PROBLEM

Document heavy workflows create operational drag that users feel every day.

The product opportunity was not to add more AI features. It was to identify where people were spending time on repetitive documentation work, automate the right moments, and preserve enough context and control for the workflow to remain trustworthy.

MY PRODUCT APPROACH

I redesigned around the work users valued, not feature volume.

01

Target high friction jobs

Prioritize the steps where automation could materially reduce time to complete instead of spreading AI across every surface.

02

Improve onboarding

Reduce the distance between first use and the moment a user understands the product’s practical value.

03

Strengthen AI context

Improve how the product used context so generated output became more relevant and dependable for the workflow.

04

Build platform foundations

Own roadmap work across integrations, APIs, authentication and shared services so enterprise adoption did not depend on isolated features.

MEASUREMENT

The success signal was whether the workflow became faster and easier to adopt.

50%+reduction in documentation time
+35%feature adoption
+40%AI accuracy / context relevance
SECOND ORDER IMPACT

Better workflow value supported broader enterprise traction.

The product work also contributed to stronger enterprise adoption and retention. I treat those as linked outcomes: when the workflow saves meaningful time, the platform foundations make it usable in enterprise contexts, and users understand the value quickly, adoption has a reason to persist.

+30%enterprise adoption
+25%retention
hours to minutesautomation turnaround in core workflows
WHAT THIS PROVES

I can connect AI capability to workflow economics.

Good AI product management is not just model quality. It is choosing where intelligence changes the job, designing the human workflow around it, and measuring whether users actually move faster or adopt more deeply.
