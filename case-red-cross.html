EVIDENCE 06 · OPERATIONAL DATA PRODUCT

Reducing reconciliation effort through better workflow and reporting.

A product case from a financial reconciliation environment: mapping operational friction, improving invoice and cash flow workflows, and using data visibility to help teams complete work faster without weakening governance.

RoleProduct Manager
DomainFinancial operations
Outcome30% reconciliation time
ToolsSQL · Power BI · Jira · Aha!
THE PROBLEM

Financial reconciliation was consuming time that should have gone to higher value operational work.

Manual invoice, cash flow and reconciliation steps created friction for finance operations and made it harder to move quickly while maintaining the governance expected in a high accountability environment.

MY PRODUCT APPROACH

I treated reporting and reconciliation as one operating workflow.

01

Map the reconciliation journey

Understand where manual handoffs, repeated checks and information gaps created the greatest operational drag.

02

Automate repeatable work

Improve invoice and cash flow workflows so users spent less time on mechanical reconciliation steps.

03

Make data decision ready

Use SQL and Power BI to improve visibility into the information teams needed to complete and review the work.

04

Build with governance in mind

Partner with finance, compliance and audit stakeholders so efficiency gains did not weaken controls or traceability.

OUTCOME

Less reconciliation effort, stronger operational visibility.

30%reduction in reconciliation time
SQLdata validation and operational analysis
Power BIreporting and decision visibility
WHAT THIS PROVES

I can lead internal products where efficiency, data and controls all matter.

Internal products deserve the same product discipline as customer facing ones: understand the job, reduce friction, preserve trust and measure whether the operating process actually improved.
