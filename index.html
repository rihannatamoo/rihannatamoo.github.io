PRODUCT JUDGMENT

What I bring when the path is not obvious.

Strong product work is not a collection of ceremonies. It is a sequence of decisions that makes the product clearer, the delivery safer and the outcome more likely.

01

Find the problem beneath the request.

Clarify the job, operating context, user evidence and business reason before prescribing the feature.

02

Make product decisions explicit.

Defaults, trade offs, exclusions, dependencies and success signals should be visible, not trapped in meetings.

03

Design the system, not only the screen.

Consider reusable capability, configuration, data, permissions, migration, operational impact and future change.

04

Stay accountable through delivery.

Requirements, QA, UAT, release readiness and measurement are part of product ownership, not somebody else’s problem.
