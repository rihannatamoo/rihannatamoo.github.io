HOW I LEAD PRODUCT

Make the product clearer. Make the team faster.

I am most useful when the problem is complex, the inputs are messy and a team needs a product model it can actually build, test and operate.

01

Define the real product problem.

Separate the request from the user job, business outcome and product constraint before deciding what should be built.

02

Turn ambiguity into a system.

Define the information model, workflows, business rules, defaults, permissions, edge cases and product boundaries.

03

Make tradeoffs visible.

Prioritize against customer value, platform integrity, technical feasibility, release risk and the cost of future change.

04

Stay accountable through delivery.

Product ownership continues through engineering, QA, UAT, launch, measurement and the next decision.
