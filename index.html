HOW I LEAD PRODUCT

Clear product decisions. Stronger execution.

I work across strategy, discovery, product definition and delivery. The goal is to make the product easier to understand, build, test and evolve.

01

Define the real problem.

Separate the request from the user job, business outcome and product constraint before deciding what should be built.

02

Design the product system.

Define the workflow, information model, business rules, defaults, permissions, edge cases and product boundaries.

03

Make tradeoffs explicit.

Prioritize against customer value, platform integrity, technical feasibility, delivery risk and the cost of future change.

04

Own the outcome through delivery.

Stay accountable through engineering, QA, UAT, launch, measurement and the decisions that follow.

PRODUCT LEADERSHIP

Strategy through delivery, without losing the thread.

StrategyTurn company direction and customer problems into product choices with a clear reason to exist.
DiscoveryStructure ambiguous problems, test assumptions and define the user job before committing to a solution.
Product definitionTranslate decisions into flows, rules, requirements and experiences teams can build and test.
DeliveryCarry the product through engineering, quality, release readiness and measurement without handing off the thinking.
