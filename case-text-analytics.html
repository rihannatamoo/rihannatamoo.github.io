WHAT I OWNED

My role was to turn the capability into a coherent enterprise product.

01

Product architecture

Separated AI synthesis, approved topic analysis, customer language exploration and emerging theme discovery so each surface has a distinct user job.

02

Information and interaction design

Defined shared context, defaults, sorting, sentiment filters, evidence drill downs, selected state and the relationships between surfaces.

03

AI explainability and governance

Kept insights traceable to comments and made discovered themes reviewable through rationale, parent suggestions, similarity context and human decision controls.

04

Delivery definition

Translated the product model into FRDs, business rules, vertical stories, acceptance criteria and edge cases for engineering and QA.

PRODUCT RULES

The screens were backed by explicit rules, not left for implementation to guess.

NPSPromoter percentage minus detractor percentage for eligible survey responses. If a topic has 52% promoters and 14% detractors, NPS displays 38.0.
Period changeThe selected period is compared with the previous equivalent period. If current volume is 120 and previous volume is 100, change displays 20.0% increase.
No eligible NPSWhen there are no eligible NPS responses, the product displays a not available state rather than implying zero.
Zero denominatorWhen the previous period total is zero, percentage change is not calculated because the result would be misleading.
