CASE STUDY 01 · AI CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE · ENTERPRISE SAAS

Turning an ambiguous AI brief into a coherent customer intelligence product.

The brief sounded simple: modernize text analytics with AI. The real work was defining what the product should become, separating overlapping concepts, creating a trustworthy path from signal to evidence, and translating the model into behavior engineering and QA could implement.

01 · CONTEXT

The existing product could classify feedback. The opportunity was to help users understand what was changing and why.

The legacy capability already processed customer comments. That meant the challenge was not starting from zero. It was deciding how a mature classification experience should evolve when AI introduces discovery, synthesis and recommendation.

INPUTS

Structured and unstructured feedback

Survey comments and review data entered through file imports or APIs, with future expansion planned for additional feedback sources.

PROCESSING

Sentiment and taxonomy matching

The current system used sentiment analysis, configured topic patterns, keywords and comment level evidence.

CURRENT VALUE

Understand known categories

Teams could see how customers were talking about topics the business had already defined and chosen to monitor.

PRODUCT GAP

Discover the unexpected

The existing model was weaker at surfacing new patterns that had not already been encoded into the taxonomy.

The product question I reframed:

How do we combine known taxonomy analysis, emerging pattern discovery, AI generated synthesis and customer comments without creating four different names for the same thing?

02 · PRODUCT DIAGNOSIS

The main risk was conceptual overlap, not lack of features.

Early concepts included topics, subtopics, themes, discovery candidates, keywords, sentiment, AI insights and customer comments. Each item sounded reasonable in isolation. Together, they created ambiguity about where a user should go for an answer and what each surface actually owned.

I used three product tests to clarify the model: what question is the user trying to answer, whether the signal is known or emerging, and what evidence the user needs before acting on it.

TEST 01

What question does this page answer?

A page needed a distinct job. If two surfaces answered the same question, the model was not yet clear enough.

TEST 02

Is the signal known or emerging?

Known taxonomy and new pattern discovery require different workflows, governance and user expectations.

TEST 03

How can the user verify it?

Every aggregate insight needed a credible path back to the customer comments that support it.

TEST 04

What happens next?

Discovery is useful only when users can interpret, approve, merge, reject, suppress or otherwise act on the signal.

03 · FUTURE STATE PRODUCT MODEL

I separated four product jobs instead of forcing everything into one analytics page.

SURFACE 01

AI Insights

Executive synthesis of what matters now.

  • Exactly three key insights
  • One positive, one neutral and one negative
  • Where to focus, suggested actions and expected impact
  • All supporting comments available with sentiment filtering
SURFACE 02

Topic Analysis

Performance of the taxonomy the business already tracks.

  • Topic and subtopic analysis
  • Sentiment distribution and NPS
  • Change versus previous period
  • Frequency as the default sort
  • Drill down to comments
SURFACE 03

Keyword Explorer

A separate way to inspect language patterns without confusing keywords with taxonomy.

  • All keywords as the default view
  • Highest volume as the default sort
  • Sentiment, NPS and period change
  • Direct drill down to supporting comments
SURFACE 04

Theme Discovery

Governed discovery for patterns that may deserve a place in the taxonomy.

  • Topic and subtopic candidates only
  • Suggested parent and closest existing topic
  • Volume, penetration and period change
  • Approve, merge, reject and suppress actions
Recreated capability model
Feedback sources→Enrichment→Known topic analysis+Emerging theme discovery+AI synthesis→Customer evidence→Decision or action
Public safe reconstruction of the product logic. No proprietary screen, customer data or internal implementation detail is shown.
04 · DECISION LOG

The quality of the product depended on dozens of small decisions being made explicit.

These decisions look tactical, but they determine whether the experience feels coherent and whether engineering and QA interpret the requirement the same way.

Decision
Reasoning
Product consequence
Keep Topic Analysis and Theme Discovery separate
Known taxonomy and emerging patterns answer different user questions and require different governance.
Users can analyze what is already tracked without confusing it with candidates that may become part of the taxonomy.
Keep Keyword Explorer as its own surface
Keywords are supporting language signals, not the same thing as topics or discovery candidates.
The information model stays clean while users still get direct access to language trends.
Show exactly three AI insights
Unlimited generated insights reduce focus and make the page feel like another report.
The system presents one positive, one neutral and one negative signal to create a balanced executive view.
Use sentiment as the comment filter
Once users drill into evidence, duplicated topic and insight selectors add friction without creating new value.
Comments remain easy to inspect while the selected context is shown above the evidence.
Add NPS and period change
Volume alone does not show business relevance or movement.
Topic and keyword views communicate scale, customer advocacy and directional change together.
05 · HUMAN GOVERNANCE FOR AI DISCOVERY

Discovery needed a lifecycle, not a magic button.

I designed the admin concept so the system can surface candidates while humans retain control over what becomes part of the managed taxonomy.

01

Observed

The system detects a recurring pattern in feedback. Observation alone does not make it a managed topic.

02

Eligible for review

A candidate reaches the configured occurrence and penetration threshold. Only eligible candidates enter the active review queue.

03

Admin decision

The reviewer can approve, merge, reject or suppress. Subtopic candidates can be assigned to or moved under a parent.

04

Future enrichment

Approved taxonomy changes affect future enrichment. The audit trail records promoted or declined decisions and who made them.

The goal is to make an AI suggestion reviewable rather than asking an administrator to trust a label with no context.
06 · FROM PRODUCT MODEL TO BUILDABLE REQUIREMENTS

I carried the decisions into rules that could be built and tested.

The FRD was not a transcript of stakeholder requests. It became the contract for how the experience should behave. I kept requirements outcome focused, placed calculations under business rules, and used acceptance criteria to remove implementation ambiguity.

NPSFor eligible survey responses, NPS is the percentage of promoters minus the percentage of detractors. Example: 52% promoters and 14% detractors results in 38.0.
Period changeCurrent period total compared with previous period total. Example: 120 versus 100 displays a 20.0% increase.
No eligible NPSWhen a keyword or topic has no eligible NPS responses, the product displays a not available state rather than implying a score of zero.
Zero denominatorWhen the previous period total is zero, percentage change is not calculated because the denominator would make the result misleading.

Build the analytics page

VERTICAL SLICE

Deliver a usable product outcome

  • Acceptance criteria written to be testable
07 · CROSS FUNCTIONAL DELIVERY

The work moved through design, stakeholder review, requirements and engineering readiness.

I used competitor validation to test the product model, not to copy screens. Qualtrics and Medallia helped validate patterns such as topic drill down, sentiment context, discovery candidates and human approval. Internal review then sharpened the experience around our own product logic.

DISCOVERY

Clarify language and user jobs

Resolve the difference between topic, theme, keyword, insight and evidence before detailing screens.

DESIGN

Reduce duplicate controls

Use the page context and selected breadcrumb to remove selectors that repeated information users already had.

REQUIREMENTS

Turn visuals into behavior

Document business rules, defaults, permissions, filters, exports, sorting, edge cases and definitions.

HANDOFF

Write for engineering and QA

Slice stories around usable outcomes so teams can build and validate the intended behavior end to end.

08 · MEASUREMENT AND CURRENT STATUS

I do not manufacture an outcome metric before a product ships.

This work is still a product definition and delivery case, so I separate evidence of product quality from post release business outcomes. The case is strong because the reasoning, artifacts and decisions are inspectable. Outcome metrics should be measured once customers are using the capability.

Time to insightHow quickly a user can move from a high level signal to the supporting customer evidence.
Insight engagementWhether AI insights are opened, investigated and connected to comments or recommended actions.
Candidate decision rateHow often eligible discovery candidates are reviewed and resolved by administrators.
Taxonomy qualityWhether approved candidates improve coverage without creating duplicate or low value categories.
Drill down behaviorWhether users inspect the evidence behind topics, keywords and AI generated insights.
Export and repeat usageWhether the capability becomes part of recurring analysis rather than a one time exploration.
CURRENT EVIDENCE

Product architecture, decision records, FRD content, business rules, acceptance criteria, story slicing and delivery readiness. Post release adoption and business outcome data will be added only when it exists.

WHAT THIS CASE PROVES

I can lead AI product work without treating AI itself as the product.

I can take a broad AI ambition, find the underlying user jobs, separate overlapping concepts, design human governance, make edge cases explicit and carry the model down to buildable, testable requirements.
