PRODUCT DECISIONS

The product works because the rules behind the screens are deliberate.

01

Separate known and emerging analysis

Topic Analysis measures what the business already tracks. Theme Discovery handles new patterns that may deserve a place in the taxonomy.

02

Keep keywords separate

Keywords are language signals, not managed topics. Giving them their own surface prevents the information model from becoming muddy.

03

Make AI explainable

Insights and themes always connect back to the underlying customer comments so users can verify the signal before acting.

04

Use business context, not volume alone

NPS and period change sit beside frequency so users can see customer advocacy and movement, not just how often something appears.

MAKING IT BUILDABLE

I took the product from concept to implementation ready behavior.

My work covered the product model, page behavior, filters, default states, calculations, drill downs, governance actions, edge cases, functional requirements and acceptance criteria needed for engineering and QA to build the experience consistently.

NPSPromoter percentage minus detractor percentage for eligible survey responses. Example: 52% promoters and 14% detractors displays 38.0.
Period changeCurrent period compared with the previous equivalent period. Example: 120 versus 100 displays a 20.0% increase.
No eligible dataWhere NPS cannot be calculated, the interface shows a not available state rather than a misleading zero.
Comment evidenceUsers can inspect all comments used by the analysis and filter the evidence by sentiment.
