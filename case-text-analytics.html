MY PRODUCT OWNERSHIP

I defined the product behavior behind the screens.

ARCHITECTURE

Separated distinct analytical jobs

Kept insights, topics, keywords and emerging themes conceptually clear so users understand what each experience is for.

EXPLAINABILITY

Made evidence part of the workflow

Designed drill downs so users can move from an aggregate signal to the comments that support it rather than trusting opaque AI output.

GOVERNANCE

Kept humans in control of taxonomy

Defined how discovered candidates are qualified, reviewed and either approved, merged, rejected or suppressed.

DELIVERY

Turned the model into buildable rules

Defined filters, default states, calculations, edge cases, functional requirements, acceptance criteria and expected interactions for engineering and QA.

PRODUCT RULES

The experience is only credible when the calculations and states are explicit.

NPSPromoter percentage minus detractor percentage for eligible survey responses. Example: 52% promoters and 14% detractors displays 38.0.
Period changeCurrent period compared with the previous equivalent period. Example: 120 versus 100 displays a 20.0% increase.
No eligible dataWhere NPS cannot be calculated, the interface shows a not available state instead of implying a score of zero.
Customer evidenceUsers can inspect the comments supporting the selected topic, keyword or AI insight and filter that evidence by sentiment.
