01 / The opportunity

One affordability problem, two sides of the market.

My market research surfaced a core barrier: customers wanted products but could not always pay the full price upfront. At the same time, merchants needed effective channels to showcase products and turn demand into completed sales.

CONSUMER NEED

Get the product without waiting to save the full price.

Allow eligible customers to purchase immediately and repay through installments instead of requiring the total payment at checkout.

MERCHANT NEED

Convert purchases without waiting for each installment.

Offer BNPL alongside a merchant e-commerce dashboard to display products. After successful transactions, merchants received their full payment.

02 / Product strategy

We had to design the whole financial journey—not just a payment button.

I helped shape the product direction across discovery, financing eligibility, checkout and repayments. Success depended on connecting consumer affordability with merchant sales while keeping payment operations workable.

04 / Senior PM decisions

Where my ownership went beyond feature delivery.

01 · MARKET RESEARCH

Define the opportunity before building

I researched upfront affordability barriers and helped shape a product proposition serving both consumers and merchants.

02 · ELIGIBILITY DESIGN

Balance access with responsible financing

I helped develop application and psychometric questions, discussed assessment criteria with the team, and validated decision logic and calculations using Postman.

03 · PAYMENTS COMPLEXITY

Connect consumer payments to merchant settlement

I worked across engineering, finance, risk, compliance and providers on transaction workflows and integration requirements.

04 · CUSTOMER CONTEXT

Make installment timing more useful

I helped introduce flexible automated repayment schedules aligned with customer earning patterns, rather than treating fixed dates as universally suitable.
