THE PRODUCT CONTEXT

Growth in lending is a system problem, not a conversion button problem.

Digital lending and BNPL products have to create value for customers and merchants while respecting risk, settlement, operational and compliance constraints. Improving one metric in isolation can damage another part of the system.

MY PRODUCT WORK

I led launches across the journey, not just one surface.

01

Expand useful entry points

Worked across BNPL, storefront, purchase link, subscription bundle and installment workflows so users and merchants had more practical ways to transact.

02

Balance growth with risk

Coordinate product decisions with engineering, data science, risk and compliance instead of treating growth as independent from portfolio quality.

03

Launch, learn, iterate

Use customer response and operating constraints to improve the product after launch rather than treating release as the end state.

04

Protect the customer experience

Measure satisfaction alongside commercial growth so expansion did not come at the expense of the user relationship.
